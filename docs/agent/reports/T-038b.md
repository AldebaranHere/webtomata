## 4. AC5, corrected by the owner's ruling

The literal check “every nonterminal in a rule body is the head of a rule” is false for the correct
T-037 grammar. The opened grammar for wcwᴿ, as measured in the e2e and with the engine:

```text
heads: 22
body nonterminals: 33
head ∩ body: 20
body nonterminals with no rule: 13
V_1gep0, V_1gep3,
V_3g0p0, V_3g0p1, V_3g0p3,
V_3g1p0, V_3g1p1, V_3g1p3,
V_3g2p0, V_3g2p1, V_3g2p3,
V_3gep0, V_3gep1
```

The 13 are the rules into the new accepting state `a` (the `V_3…` variables on the right) and two
unreachable `V_1gep…` variables; the T-037 output is the pre-cleanup grammar. The corrected AC5
check is in `pda-conversion.wasm.test.ts`:

1. the opened grammar’s head set equals the engine grammar’s;
2. its body-variable set equals the engine grammar’s;
3. the **head/body intersection** equals the engine grammar’s — **20**, where the `<q,A,r>` text
   would give **0**;
4. it round-trips to the same **76-rule multiset**.

The guard break above shows (3) failing with `[]` when the body is written in the pretty names. The
e2e of AC5 checks the rest: `?mode=grammar`, title `wcwR (CFG)`, the selected custom grammar, the
76 rules, the `V_…` text, and the source PDA record unchanged.
