## Focus on the phone

- After the delete the focus goes to the next card's `.card-select` (or the previous one when the deleted card was last, or `+ Tata Bahasa Baru` when the list is empty). It is **not** lost to `<body>`, and the e2e asserts it on both projects.
- **The toast can sit on top of that card.** On a phone the focused card is behind the fixed toast whenever it is the last row of the page, and no scroll can lift it: the last row is the end of the document, so `scrollIntoView({ block: 'center' })` has nowhere to go. The same happens on the laptop for the last row of the library: measured `scrollTop` 436, `scrollHeight` 1154, `clientHeight` 718 — already at the maximum, and a manual `scrollIntoView({ block: 'center' })` changes nothing. When there is content below the focused card, `revealAboveToast()` does scroll it into view; when the card is the last row, the ring stays behind the toast for the 6 seconds. A tap on a covered row waits the same 6 seconds. This is the trade the planner accepted ("it can cover the bottom card row, as the editors' toast covers the canvas bottom"); it is bigger on the phone, where the covered row is the row the toast is about.
- The phone tab is held on **Pustaka** after the delete and after Urungkan (lines 499 and 523). Without it, deleting the **selected** grammar calls `selectPreset`, which sets `phoneTab = 'editor'` (line 436) and hides the Pustaka pane — the toast with Urungkan would be invisible — and the same for `selectCustom` inside Urungkan (line 449), which would hide the pane before the focus could move to the restored card.
- On the phone the action was a tap, so Chrome paints no `:focus-visible` ring on the moved focus; the blue border in `phone-toast.png` is the *selected* card's accent border peeking around the toast, not a ring.

## The undo, its drop sites, and the live region

- Value: `undoDelete = { grammar: { ...grammar }, index, wasSelected }` — a plain copy, one value, in the component. Restore: `[...customGrammars.slice(0, at), pending.grammar, ...customGrammars.slice(at)]` with `at = Math.min(pending.index, customGrammars.length)` (the index never exceeds the list length because every other change drops the undo).
- Urungkan saves to `localStorage` again, and selects the grammar again **only** when `wasSelected` and `selectedId` is still `GRAMMAR_PRESETS[0].id` (line 520).
- Drops: the delete replaces the previous undo; `createNewCustomGrammar` (459); `handleOpenCnfAsNewGrammar` (473); the `openRequest` effect, i.e. the third `nextCustomId` caller (296); `onMatrixChange` **only when the custom grammar's text really changed** (`target.grammar !== text`, 744), so editing a preset does not drop it; the 6 s timeout and `ondismiss` (`ondismiss={dropUndo}`); and Urungkan itself. Leaving the workstation needs no code: the sandbox page destroys the component (`{:else if activeMode === 'grammar'}`), and the timer dies with it.
- The live region is the editors' own `Announcer.svelte`, imported unchanged and rendered once as a child of `.workstation` (835) so that it is outside the tab panes and is never `display:none`. It says `grammar.deleted_custom { title }` at a delete and `editor.undone` after Urungkan (no new text for the undo; "Dibatalkan." is already what the editors say, `editor.ts:686`). The toast itself is not a live region, so nothing is said twice.

## Limits and boundaries

- The 6 s limit: tested at 5,999 ms (the toast is there) and 6,000 ms (gone), not at a far-over value (AC4).
- The index of the restore: middle (AC1), first (AC5, twice), last (AC6), and the empty list → `+ Tata Bahasa Baru` (AC6). The clamp is `Math.min(pending.index, customGrammars.length)`.
- One undo: a second delete replaces the first (AC5); a lowered id (`G2` freed, then `+ New` reuses `G1`… in the test the freed `G1` is reused) does not come back (AC5).
- A reload inside the 6 s: the deletion is already in the storage (AC4); Urungkan before 6 s survives a reload (AC4).
- Leaving the workstation: the undo and the toast die with the component (AC8).
